## Architecture rules (keep)
- **Permissions**: registry in `src/lib/permissions.ts`; role→permission rows live in the DB and are editable at Settings → Roles. `isSuperAdmin` roles always pass. Every mutation is a server action wrapped in `guarded(permission, …)` (`src/lib/action.ts`); pages use `requirePagePermission`. Never rely on hidden UI.
- **Sessions**: DB-backed, cookie holds a random token, only its SHA-256 is stored; 12 h; deactivated users lose access immediately; 5 failed logins → 15 min lock.
- **Money** `Decimal(14,2)`, **quantities** `Decimal(14,3)`, unit factors `Decimal(14,4)`. Use `Prisma.Decimal`, never JS floats. Currency is TZS (0 decimals), stored in `Setting`, treated as fixed.
- **Stock is always in the product's base unit.** Packaging (carton = 12 bottles) lives in `ProductUnit.factor`; convert with `src/lib/units.ts` (rejects results that would need rounding). Base unit cannot change after creation. Product forms never set stock; stock changes only through stock movements (Phase 2+).
- **Stock engine** (`src/lib/inventory/engine.ts`): EVERY quantity change goes through `moveStock()` inside a `db.$transaction` — it atomically updates `StockBalance` (`UPDATE … WHERE quantity+delta >= 0`, so concurrent sales cannot oversell) and appends the `StockMovement` ledger row (signed qty, previous/new, unit cost, reference, user). Never update `StockBalance` or `Product.avgCost` any other way. Negative stock is **not** supported (DB CHECK); allowing it later means dropping that CHECK and adding a setting.
- Posted stock documents (`StockReceipt`, `StockTransfer`, `StockAdjustment` + items) and `StockMovement` are immutable (DB triggers). Fix mistakes with a new adjustment, never an edit. Documents carry a unique `submissionKey` (blocks double submits) and numbers from `nextDocumentNo` (GRN/TRF/ADJ-YYYY-000001; reuse for sales/payments).
- Lock order matters: sort lines by productId; `lockProducts` before reading/writing `avgCost`; transfers lock the two balance rows in locationId order. **Phase 3 sales** must call `moveStock(type SOLD)` and snapshot `Product.avgCost` as COGS under a product row lock (`FOR SHARE`/`FOR UPDATE`) so a concurrent receipt can't skew it.
- Costing = **weighted average per product across all locations** (`Product.avgCost`, 4 dp), updated only by receipts and stock-increasing adjustments. Receiving cost needs `products.cost`; without it stock is received at the product's `purchasePrice` (must be > 0). Damaged stock is removed from sellable stock as a `DAMAGED` movement valued at average cost.
- Permissions added in Phase 2: `inventory.movements` (view ledger/documents). A permission that is new in `permissions.ts` is auto-granted by the seed to built-in roles that list it (existing owner edits untouched).
- **Sales** (`src/lib/sales/post-sale.ts`, Phase 3): one transaction posts invoice + lines + stock (`SOLD` movements) + payment + allocation. Lock order: `INV` counter → customer row → products `FOR SHARE` → stock balances → `PAY` counter. Phase 4 payment collection must take the **customer row lock before anything else** and allocate through `PaymentAllocation`, updating `Sale.amountPaid`/`paymentStatus` in the same transaction (DB CHECKs reject an inconsistent status).
- **Money model**: a `Sale` is the invoice (revenue recognised once). Cash received is a `Payment` linked by `PaymentAllocation`; collecting on a credit invoice later must NEVER create a new sale. What a customer owes is always calculated (`customerOutstanding`), never stored. Payment *type* (full/part/credit) is separate from payment *method* (cash/mobile money/bank). Zero-total sales are not allowed (a zero amount never counts as paid).
- **Collections** (`src/lib/sales/collections.ts`, Phase 4): `postCollection` allocates one payment over chosen invoices (never above an invoice's balance; extra only if the person explicitly keeps it as customer credit). Advance credit = unallocated part of a customer's payments (`customerCredit`); `postCreditApplication` spends it on invoices (no new cash). All of it runs under the customer-row lock first (then invoice rows → `PAY` counter), same as sales. DB triggers: an allocation can't exceed its payment and must match the invoice's customer; at COMMIT every sale's `amountPaid` must equal the sum of its allocations. Statement balance = invoices − payments (negative = paid in advance).
- Prices: the server recomputes list prices from the DB (`listPrice`: unit override → base × factor; wholesale/distributor customers get wholesale price, falling back to retail). Browser only sends "what and how many". Charging below list needs `sales.discount`; above list is refused. Credit sales need `sales.credit`, a customer, and a credit limit > 0; going over the limit needs `credit.approve_exception` + a reason (audited as `credit.exception_approved`). Sales are never deleted or edited (DB trigger); cancellations/returns/refunds are a later phase.
- Salespeople without `sales.view_all` only ever see their own sales (other people's invoices return 404). Cost/profit on a sale is shown only with `dashboard.financials`.
- **Multiple stock locations** are supported from the start (`Location`; one default, enforced by a partial unique index).
- **No hard deletes** of business records; deactivate (`status`) or reverse. `AuditLog` is append-only (DB trigger blocks UPDATE/DELETE/TRUNCATE). Write audit rows in the same transaction as the change (`writeAudit(tx, …)`).
- DB CHECK constraints (non-negative prices, factor > 0) are in the init migration SQL; keep new guards there too.
- No sparkle/star icons anywhere. Brand colours: navy `#0E3D6E`, red `#ED1C24`, oil yellow `#FDC400`.
- Product photos in `PRODUCTS /` are reference images only; do not seed products from them.

## Shipments / receiving document (done, 2026-11-05)
- Flow (design in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` §10b): owner announces a `Shipment` (`shipments.manage`: reference, expected date, lines with cost; can create a **new product with picture right in the form**) → nothing changes in stock → attendant (`shipments.receive`, sees **no costs**) counts good/damaged per line, plus unexpected items, as a draft (`saveDraft`) and `confirmSession`s (several sessions allowed; "nothing more is coming" closes it and records `missingQty`) → owner is notified; any damaged/missing/extra/unexpected line sets `issue = OPEN` until the owner closes the investigation (`resolveIssue`, note mandatory).
- Code: `src/lib/shipments/engine.ts` (all logic; tests in `shipments.db.test.ts`), `src/app/(app)/shipments/*`, `src/components/shipments/*`. Lock order: shipment row → `GRN` counter → products → balances. Confirm posts a normal `StockReceipt` via `postReceipt` (good + damaged), then a `DAMAGED` movement for the damaged part; double-confirm is blocked by the shipment row lock.
- Cost: announced line cost sets weighted `avgCost` on receipt. **Unexpected items enter stock at cost 0 and do not touch `avgCost`** — the owner must set their cost/price when closing the investigation.
- **Flow update (Nino, 2026-11-06)**: the shop must press **Mark as arrived** (`markArrived`, `Shipment.arrivedAt/arrivedById`, audited, owner notified) before anything can be counted; the owner cannot cancel once it has arrived. The owner enters per line the **buying price** (per piece or line total), optional **selling and wholesale price** (stored on `ShipmentItem.sellingPrice/wholesalePrice`, needs `products.price`; the new prices go live, with price history, when that line is first counted in, once) and can create a new product with its picture in the form. Stock overview shows stock value, selling value and expected profit with `inventory.valuation`.
- After adding permissions to `permissions.ts`, run `npm run db:seed` on every database so built-in roles get them (the seed grants new permissions to roles that list them).

## Cash, expenses, deposits and daily closing (done, 2026-11-06)
- **Ledger**: `CashTransaction` (append-only, signed, DB-guarded) per `CashAccount` (CASH / MOBILE_MONEY / BANK per location). Balances are always `SUM(amount)`, never stored. Rows are written **in the same transaction as the source record**: `recordPaymentCash` (post-sale, collections), `recordRefundCash` (reversal approval), expenses, deposits, manual entries, closing difference. Unique (account, type, referenceType, referenceId) = nothing counted twice. Credit sales and credit applications write nothing. Code: `src/lib/cash/*` (ledger, expenses, deposits, closing, settings, attachments); tests `cash.db.test.ts`.
- **Accounts** (Nino, 2026-11-07: "when selling, choose which payment method was used: bank → the bank, Lipa → Lipa"): a location has ONE till (cash) and any number of mobile-money / bank accounts, each with name, number and holder (`CashAccount.accountNumber/holderName/status/isDefault`; DB guards: one till per location, one default per method). The Accounts page (`/cash`, menu name "Accounts"; Nino: "only the accounts, the transactions inside each account", "no empty spaces") shows one All money total and the account cards in a grid whose last card fills its row (no gaps); "Add account" is a button on top (`/cash/new`, then it opens the new account); each opens `/cash/[id]` with its own balance, every movement of money in and out with the balance after each entry (a window SUM over the account's rows), type/date filters, Record an entry, Edit, Make main and Close. The owner adds, edits and closes accounts there (`cash.adjust`; a closed account must hold nothing and cannot be the main one). Every flow that moves money takes an optional `accountId` resolved by `resolveAccount()` (`src/lib/cash/ledger.ts`): an explicit account must match the location and method and be open; without one the default is used ONLY when the method has a single open account, otherwise the person must choose. Refunds are lenient (they leave the account the customer paid into; expense reversals go back where the expense came from). POS and Record payment show "Paid by" buttons per account (`AccountButtons`); expenses, supplier payments, deposits (into a chosen bank), the closing deposit and manual entries use `AccountSelect`. A payment's account is the account of its ledger row (the posted Payment is immutable and stores nothing); `Expense.cashAccountId` keeps the choice for expenses that wait for approval. Daily closing still counts only the till. The shop's accounts (Cash, M-PESA 37453908, CRDB, NMB) were set up in the LOCAL dev DB by SQL; new databases start with default accounts called Cash on hand / Mobile money / Bank account, which the owner renames and numbers in Cash & Accounts (Manage).
- **Expenses**: **no approval is needed by default** (Nino, 2026-11-07: "there is no expense that needs the boss's approval for now"): an expense is recorded and paid at once (ledger row written). Only when the owner sets a limit above 0 (Setting `expenseApprovalLimit`, or a category's own limit) does an expense above it wait as PENDING, with **no money moving** until `expenses.approve` approves it. Approved expenses are never edited/deleted; `reverseExpense` puts the money back. Spending is refused if the account balance is too low (deposits, withdrawals, expenses; not refunds).
- **Deposit** = transfer (DEPOSIT_OUT cash + DEPOSIT_IN bank), partial allowed, reference required, optional slip photo (`Attachment`, resized WebP, `/api/attachment/[id]`).
- **Daily closing** (`submitClosing`): locks the cash account row, takes every open ledger row up to the day (a row belongs to ONE closing), expected = opening + in − out, books `CLOSING_DIFFERENCE` so the ledger equals the counted cash (carry-forward), optional deposit in the same transaction, then locks the figures (DB trigger). Difference above Setting `closingTolerance` → status NEEDS_REVIEW (owner is notified; sign-off needs a note). Corrections after closing = `cash.adjust` entries. Lock order: cash account → document counters. One closing per location per day; later days cannot be closed before earlier ones.
- Permissions: `expenses.approve`, `cash.view`, `cash.adjust` (management); `deposits.record` (shop-floor, never implied by Super Admin). Owner sets the opening balance once per account in Cash & Accounts before the first closing. Seed creates the 3 accounts and 6 starting expense categories (only when none exist).

## Resellers / consignment (done, 2026-11-06)
- **Issue = custody transfer, not a sale**: `issueStock` moves stock from the shop location to the reseller's own `Location` (type RESELLER, created with the reseller), records a `ResellerIssue` with the agreed prices (wholesale price, retail when none; snapshot per entered unit and per base unit), no cash, no receivable. Custody stock is excluded from the shop shelf (stock overview default, low-stock alerts, location pickers) and shown as "With resellers"; inventory value includes it.
- **Sold report = the sale**: `reportSold` calls `postSale` with `reseller: {id, basePrices}` → credit invoice (`Sale.channel = RESELLER`, `resellerId`) on the reseller's `Customer` (type WHOLESALE, credit limit 0, not enforced for reseller sales), stock leaves the **custody** location, price = latest agreed base price × unit factor; a lower price needs `sales.discount` + a reason; higher is refused. Due date = `Reseller.settlementDays`. Revenue is recognised here once.
- **Money in** = ordinary collection (`/customers/[customerId]/pay`, `payments.record`) allocated to the reseller's invoices, lands in the cash ledger. **Returns**: good goods custody → shop transfer, damaged ones written off from custody (`ResellerReturn`); normal sale reversals of reseller invoices put goods back into custody. Identity always holds: issued − sold − returned = custody balance.
- Permissions: `resellers.view`, `resellers.manage` (register/edit/deactivate), `resellers.operate` (shop-floor: issue, sold report, returns). Code: `src/lib/resellers/engine.ts` (+ `getResellerAccount`, `getResellerProducts`), `src/app/(app)/resellers/*`, tests `resellers.db.test.ts`. Issue document is printable (`/resellers/issues/[id]`).

## Full-screen, app-like phones (Nino 2026-11-09: "the screen full like a full app, on any phone, also after I log in")
- **A web page in a browser tab can never hide the browser's address bar** (Safari and Chrome draw it, no CSS or meta tag removes it). The only way to a real full-screen app is to put the site on the home screen: the manifest (`display: standalone`, `id`, icons) and the iOS meta (`apple-mobile-web-app-capable`, `black-translucent`) then make it open with no browser bar, edge to edge. Everything below makes that installed app, and the tab, feel native.
- **Install help** (`src/components/InstallApp.tsx`): phones (width < 1024 and touch) that have not installed it see a small sheet: one **Install the app** button when the browser offers it (Android, `beforeinstallprompt`, kept at module level), else three steps (iPhone: Share, Add to Home Screen, Add; Android menu steps). It opens by itself once, 2.5 s after the first screen after sign-in (`InstallPrompt` in `AppShell`; "Got it" or closing is remembered in localStorage `sdoInstallSeen`), from **Get the full-screen app** under Log in on the sign-in page, and from **Get the app** in the person's menu (`UserMenu` on a phone, `SidebarFooter` in the drawer). Nothing shows on a computer or once installed (`display-mode: standalone` / `navigator.standalone`).
- **Status bar / notch**: the installed app runs under the phone's status bar. `.status-strip` (root layout, `globals.css`) is a fixed bar of `env(safe-area-inset-top)` height in the status bar's colour (navy in the light look, the page colour in the dark look) so the white clock is always readable; it has no height in a browser tab. The phone top card starts below it (`pt-[calc(env(safe-area-inset-top,0px)+0.75rem)]`); any new fixed or sticky element at the top of a phone screen must add `env(safe-area-inset-top)` too, and anything fixed at the bottom `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` (the bottom bar, bottom sheets and the sign-in footer already do).
- **Bar colours follow the look**: `generateViewport` in the root layout reads the `sd-theme` cookie and sets `theme-color` (`THEME_COLOR` in `src/lib/theme-client.ts`: dark `#0a1220`, light `#f4f6f9`), the sign-in page is always `#061a33`; every light / dark switch goes through `applyTheme()` (class, cookie and the meta tag), never by hand.
- **Launch screen**: `apple-touch-startup-image` for 13 iPhone sizes (`public/brand/splash/<w>x<h>@<ratio>.png`, navy with the logo, made by `scripts/make-brand-images.mjs`; the size list is also in `layout.tsx`), so an iPhone shows navy instead of a white flash while the app opens. A new iPhone size needs a line in both lists and a re-run of the script.
- **Touch behaviour** (`globals.css`): `touch-action: manipulation` (no double-tap zoom), buttons / tabs / menu links are not selectable by a long press, and on phones every field is 16 px (iPhone Safari zooms the page when a smaller field is tapped and then stays zoomed). Print and the computer layout are untouched.
